How An Order Moves

From field to freight, in four checked steps.

01 / SOURCING

Farm-direct procurement

Produce and grain are procured directly from our grower network in the Nashik–Pune corridor, keeping the chain short and traceable.

02 / GRADING & QC

Sort, clean, verify

Every lot is graded for size and quality, cleaned to export tolerance, and checked against moisture and residue standards.

03 / PACKAGING

Export-grade packing

Packed to destination requirements — vented cartons for fresh produce, PP or jute bagging for grains and pulses.

04 / EXPORT

Documented dispatch

Shipped FCL/LCL with full export documentation — invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and phytosanitary certificate.
